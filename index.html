03 / HOW IT WORKS

A short safety loop, not a trading loop.

JANCH focuses on the moment before a user trusts a claim, installs an app, shares documents or sends money.

INPUT message / link / image / voice
EXTRACT claims + entities
VERIFY official evidence
ACT pause / verify / report
Capture the suspicious contentPaste text, a URL, screenshot or use browser voice input.
Find fraud & misinformation patternsGuaranteed returns, impersonation, urgency, payment requests, private-group signals and suspicious links.
Route to trusted sourcesConnect detected concerns to official investor guidance and verification/reporting routes.
Explain what to verify nextClear actions without stock tips, price predictions or financial-product promotion.
